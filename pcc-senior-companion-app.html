🏠 Home Safety Tips
Simple reminders to help you stay safe and comfortable at home every day.
💡
Keep Walkways Clear
Remove loose rugs, cords, or clutter from hallways and between rooms. Falls are the #1 home danger for seniors — a clear path is your best protection.
🚿
Bathroom Safety
Use a non-slip bath mat inside and outside the tub or shower. If available, always use grab bars when getting in or out. Take your time — there's no rush.
💧
Stay Hydrated
Seniors often feel less thirsty but still need plenty of water. Aim for 6–8 glasses daily. Dehydration can cause dizziness and confusion that feels like something more serious.
🌙
Nighttime Safety
Keep a nightlight on in the hallway and bathroom. If you need to get up at night, sit on the edge of the bed for a few seconds before standing — this prevents dizzy spells.
🔑
Lock Up Each Night
Make a habit of checking your front door, back door, and ground-floor windows before bed. A simple checklist posted by your bedroom door can help make it routine.
📞
Phone Within Reach
Always keep your phone nearby — especially in the bathroom and bedroom. In an emergency, your phone is your lifeline. Consider a phone lanyard or belt clip.
🚨 Scam Alerts
Active warnings for scams targeting seniors in our area right now.
⚠️ High Alert — Active Now
Medicare Card Replacement Scam
Callers claiming to be Medicare say your card is expiring and they need your Social Security number and bank info to send a new one. Medicare will NEVER call you asking for this.
🛑 Rule: Hang up. Call Medicare directly at 1-800-MEDICARE.
⚠️ High Alert — Spreading Fast
Grandchild Emergency Scam
You receive a frantic call from someone pretending to be your grandchild saying they're in trouble and need you to wire money or buy gift cards immediately. They ask you not to tell anyone.
🛑 Rule: Hang up and call your grandchild directly on their real number.
⚡ Medium Alert
Computer Virus Pop-Up Scam
A scary pop-up says your computer has a virus and tells you to call a number immediately. This is fake. Closing the window or turning off your computer will fix it.
💡 Rule: Close the browser. Don't call the number. Call Pampered Companion Care if you need help.
⚡ Medium Alert
IRS Tax Debt Phone Call
Callers say you owe back taxes and will be arrested if you don't pay immediately by gift card or wire transfer. The real IRS only contacts you by mail first.
💡 Rule: The IRS never calls first. Hang up immediately.
Suspicious Call Checker
🤔 Not Sure If a Call Is a Scam?
Did they ask for gift cards, wire transfer, or your Social Security number?
Did they say you'd be arrested, fined, or lose benefits if you don't act now?
Did they ask you to keep the call secret from family or friends?
Did they call you out of the blue — you weren't expecting this call?
